## New

- **The Windows app updates itself.** Until now **Payloads & Homebrews → Updates** could replace
  every payload and homebrew except the one thing running it, so a PC could take an update, report
  the new version for your console, and go on being the older app. Updating the PKG MUTANT SHOP
  tile now replaces the Windows app as well, and tells you to close it and open it again. Nothing
  restarts on its own.

## Fixed

- **One download no longer empties the panel.** On a PC that did not hold the payload files yet,
  taking a single update could leave the panel showing only that one file, with **Nothing here for
  this console** under the other tab.
