## New

- **Updates moved into the Payloads & Homebrews header.** A dropdown beside the close button shows
  what has a newer version, with a button per item and **Update all**. Downloads are checked before
  anything is replaced, and the file being replaced is kept as `.bak` until the new one is proven
  complete.
- **The app creates its own folders.** Your games folders and the payloads folder are made on
  startup if they are missing, instead of the app telling you they are not there.

## Fixed

- A payload the console was plainly running could be shown as unreachable.
- A second PC reported "not on this PC" for files it was carrying, and could not be offered files
  another PC on the network had.
- Everything now reports which machine can supply a file, rather than only whether this one can.
