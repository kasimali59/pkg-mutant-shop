## Fixed

- **Taking an update no longer empties the Payloads & Homebrews panel.** On a PC that did not
  already have the files, downloading one of them made the panel show only that one — both tabs
  then read "Nothing here for this console". The panel now always lists everything the app knows
  about, and says for each one whether it is on this PC, on the console, or on another PC.
